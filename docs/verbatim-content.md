## /learn-to-tango (Beginners)

**Hero H1:** Learn to tango…

**Intro:**
> **Argentine Tango** is an improvised, spontaneous dance based on variations of walking, turning, pausing and adornments.
>
> Both partners bring their own signature to the dance, which fuels the unpredictability of the experience. For this reason, Tango is often described as "**a pure form of communication**" or "**a conversation without words**". Partners are responding to one another's interpretations through the connection of their bodies, not their words.

**Hashtag:** `#無基礎tango學習小組`

**Section:** Learn to tango: Beginners

**For Whom:** The Introduction to Tango class is tailored for absolute beginners and recent beginners embarking on their tango journey, with no prior experience required.

**Class Focus:** This class centres on establishing a solid foundation and introducing the essential aspects of Argentine tango including proper posture, leading and following techniques, tango walk and fundamental movements. You will also learn about the tango line of dance and experience both open and closed embraces.

**Dress Code:** Comfortable clothes and leather sole shoes for easy pivoting

No need to bring a partner with you; partners are rotated during each class.

**Student Practice Session**
Complementing the classes, there are optional practica sessions scheduled before or after class.

We believe that practice is essential to reinforce, refresh and help your body and mind retain the material covered in class.

During these sessions, you will be guided through recommended exercises while having the opportunity to ask questions, seek guidance on the movements you've learned, and discussing any challenges you may encounter during your learning journey

**Pull quote:** "Tango is a language - you begin with the alphabet, then grow your vocabulary, master the grammar, infuse your personal touch, and craft your own unique pieces..."

## /body-conditioning (Lady Tech)

**Hero H1:** Learn to tango…

**Hashtag:** `#小紅帽tango學習小組`

**Section:** Body Conditioning: Lady Tech Class

**For Whom:** This class focuses on improving body awareness and control. It is ideal for those looking to refine their alignment, flexibility, and muscle engagement. It also serves as preparation for stage tango training.

**Class Focus:** This ongoing, slow-paced class emphasises building a strong foundation for body control. A brief overview of the class:

- **Stretching:** Focused stretching exercises to improve flexibility, going beyond basic warm-ups to work deliberately on increasing range of motion. This is tailored to your individual body, respecting its limits while working toward your personal best.
- **Strength Building:** Slow and controlled Pilates-inspired body conditioning exercises to develop strength. These are not high-intensity workouts but emphasise proper alignment and targeted muscle engagement.
- **Movement Articulation in Heels:** The class also focuses on refining movements and articulations in heels, focusing on precision and fluidity.

**Dress Code:** Be prepared for floor exercises—wear pants, socks, and bring your tango heels!

We've chosen not to offer package booking because our class schedule can be irregular, and we don't wish to hold your prepayment. Instead, we'd like to recognise hardworking students and encourage attendance through the 勤學獎勵.

If you attended all classes in the same level in the previous calendar month, you'll receive 10% off when booking the same level classes in the following month.

## /meet-the-team-eugene-cor

### Meet Eugene and Cor,

**Eugene** discovered Argentine Tango in 2009. He was drawn to the way tango brings people together through music, movement and the embrace. His interest has taken him to festivals, competitions and tango communities in Hong Kong, across Asia and in Buenos Aires.

Through years of study and practice, Eugene has developed an approach grounded in partner communication, musical awareness and the possibilities of improvisation.

**Cor**'s movement background began with Chinese dance, ballet and jazz. She discovered Argentine Tango in 2010 and was drawn to its contrasts: structure and freedom, individuality and partnership, stillness and movement.

Her tango practice explores how dancers can adapt to each other through the embrace while retaining their own balance, expression and identity. Cor is also an active tango DJ, sharing the music of Argentine Tango at milongas in Hong Kong.

*photo credit: @kappu chino*

### Their tango journey

Eugene and Cor studied and developed their Tango with Argentine, international and local tango masters. They won the Hong Kong Tango Championship (Pista category) in 2017, and the China Tango Championship (Pista category) in 2018. They represented China to compete in the Mundial Tango Championship in Buenos Aires that same year.

Following their passion for Tango, they travelled to Buenos Aires in 2019 and spent eight months there immersing themselves in the culture, enjoy the milongas, and learn from the very best. They performed in 'Show de fin de año' by Pablo Giorgini and Noelia Coletti at the Teatro Empire in Buenos Aires.

In recent years, Eugene and Cor have continued to develop their tango through performance, competition and cultural exchange across Asia. Their journey has taken them to theatres, festivals and milongas in Hong Kong, Shanghai, Taiwan and South Korea. Highlights include performing with Orquesta La Juan D'Arienzo at the Taiwan Tango Marathon, as well as appearances at the Shanghai International Tango Festival, National Taichung Theater and Oppa Milonga at Tango O Nada in Seoul. Closer to home, they have performed at Bluemoon Milonga, High Tea Milonga and Peanut Milonga, at venues including the Fringe Club, Hong Kong Design Hub and The Langham. In 2026, they won the Escenario category at the Hong Kong Tango Championship and were named First Runner-Up at the China Tango Championship.

They believe that tango is a lifelong learning process and they look forward to sharing the joy of tango with more friends along the way. They are keen to share their passion and knowledge not only in the technique of the dance, but also on the understanding of the culture and philosophy.
