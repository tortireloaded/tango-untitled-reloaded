### Meet Joyce,

Joyce discovered Argentine Tango in 2010 and was immediately drawn to its musicality, emotion, and human connection. Since then, she has immersed herself in tango communities around the world through festivals, workshops, marathons, and milongas.

She has competed in Tango de Pista and Tango Escenario across Hong Kong, China, Korea, and Japan. Beyond tango, she also practices yoga, Chinese classical dance, contortion, and Pilates.

### Tango journey

Joyce and her partner Arnaud have trained with Argentine, international, and local maestros, continually developing their connection, musicality, and expression. In 2017, they travelled to Buenos Aires to study tango and experience its culture at its source.

**Their competition highlights include:**

- 3rd Place, Korea International Tango Championship Formation (2018)
- Champion, Tango Escenario, OtroTango Hong Kong (2025)
- 2nd Place, Tango Escenario, OtroTango Hong Kong (2026)
- Finalist, Tango Escenario, Mundial de Tango BA Chengdu (2026)
- Semi-finalist, Tango Escenario, Mundial de Tango BA Korea (2026)

They have also performed in Hong Kong and Seoul as both Formation members and an Escenario couple.

For Joyce, Tango is a lifelong journey of artistry, connection, and growth, and she hopes to share the beauty, presence, and authentic connection that make Argentine Tango so special.
