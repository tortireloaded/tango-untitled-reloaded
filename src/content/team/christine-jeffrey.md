### Meet Christine and Jeffrey,

Christine began her tango journey in 2011, captivated by its intricate footwork, dramatic flair, and rich emotional and cultural layers. She has continued to hone her skills under the guidance of various tango masters, enriching her tango journey through performances and competitions. Christine also enjoys traveling to connect with dancers around the world, finding joy in using tango as a universal language that bridges different cultures.

Jeffrey discovered Argentine tango in 2012, drawn to its deep philosophy of connection, presence, and the subtle art of being with others. For him, tango is more than a dance — it's an exploration of trust, communication, and shared experience. Since 2020, he has been teaching with a focus on cultivating not just strong technique, but also the emotional depth and harmony that make tango a truly transformative art form.

### Their tango journey

Together, Christine and Jeffrey took the stage in 2018 as part of a Tango Formation that earned 3rd place at the Korea International Tango Championship. In 2023, they were awarded champions of the Tango de Pista and Tango Vals Open Category at Championship organised by OtroTango in Hong Kong. In 2026, Jeffrey was also awarded First Runner-Up in the Tango de Pista category at the Hong Kong Tango Championship.

Their recent performances have taken them to Taichung Tango Academy, Oppa Milonga at Tango O Nada in Seoul, and Bluemoon Milonga at Hong Kong Design Hub.

Jeffrey and Christine continue to grow as tango dancers, performers, and teachers — sharing their love for Argentine tango's connection, creativity, and quiet magic.
