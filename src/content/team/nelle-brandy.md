### Meet Nelle and Brandy,

Nelle is a passionate dancer with a diverse background in arts spanning fashion, beauty, and various forms of movement. She first explored dance through jazz, where she developed a love for rhythm and expression. In mid-2020, she was drawn to the world of Argentine tango and has been exploring it ever since, alongside her husband, Brandy.

Brandy, an experienced interior designer, has always been deeply connected to the creative process. With over a decade of taichi practice, he brings an innate understanding of balance, fluidity, and body awareness to his dance. When he and Nelle discovered tango, they were captivated by its artistry, depth, and the unique connection it fosters.

*photo credit: @psso_studio*

Their approach to tango is shaped by their artistic sensibilities — Nelle's appreciation for movement and music, and Brandy's keen eye for design and form. They believe in learning through exploration and practice, drawing inspiration from the nuances of tango's music, culture, and embrace.

In 2026, Nelle and Brandy were awarded Second Runner-Up in the Tango de Escenario category at the Hong Kong Tango Championship.

Performing both as a duo and with the Tango Untitled team, they have appeared at the National Taichung Theater and Taichung Tango Academy in Taiwan, Oppa Milonga at Tango O Nada in Seoul, and Bluemoon Milonga at the Fringe Club and Hong Kong Design Hub, as well as Peanut Milonga at The Langham in Hong Kong.

Nelle and Brandy continue to share their discoveries and experiences with others. Join them in exploring the beauty of Argentine tango and the joy of movement!
