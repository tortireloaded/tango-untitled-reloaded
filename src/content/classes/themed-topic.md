## For Whom

The themed topic class caters to tango dancers of varying experience levels. It is recommended that class participants have a good understanding of leading, following, and dance floor navigation. Depending on the theme, additional experience may be recommended.

## Class Focus

These classes are designed to discuss, drill and refine a specific tango theme. Each themed topic series explores a focused aspect of tango, whether it's focusing on precise footwork, understanding tango movement structures, enhancing musicality, or delving into distinct tango styles. Join us in these specialised classes to expand your tango horizons, refine your skills, and add a touch of different flair to your dancing.

> "Tango is a language — you begin with the alphabet, then grow your vocabulary, master the grammar, infuse your personal touch, and craft your own unique pieces..."
