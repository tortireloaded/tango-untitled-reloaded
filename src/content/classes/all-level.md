## For Whom

Our All-Level class is suitable for those who have completed the Intro class seeking to progress further, as well as anyone looking to refresh and refine their basic skills. We are committed to the continual refinement of technique.

## Class Focus

This class places a strong emphasis on honing basic skills and their practical application. We'll engage in exercises that enhance your connection with your partner and work on simple tango figures and sequences. This class emphasises dancing to the music and we will drill on basic navigation techniques for confidently moving around the dance floor. This is an ongoing class with a different topic each time. Nothing fancy, but covering how we dance in a milonga.

## Student Practice Session

Complementing the classes, there are optional practica sessions scheduled before or after class.

We believe that practice is essential to reinforce, refresh and help your body and mind retain the material covered in class.

During these sessions, you will be guided through recommended exercises while having the opportunity to ask questions, seek guidance on the movements you've learned, and discussing any challenges you may encounter during your learning journey.

> "Tango is a language — you begin with the alphabet, then grow your vocabulary, master the grammar, infuse your personal touch, and craft your own unique pieces..."
